About

Corporate Profile

A diversified holding company with a focus on long-term value creation

The Express Airways Group is a private holding company that builds, acquires, and operates businesses across technology, media, logistics, and gaming. The group is comprised of Express Airways (International) and Explore Airways (Domestic). Founded with a mission to identify and nurture high-potential enterprises, providing them with strategic capital, operational expertise, and access to a global network.

Through its infrastructure, The Express Airways Group has collected over 1,000 singular data points, sponsors 2 strategic partnerships, and hosts 4 websites securely, all run by a lean team of 4. The group takes a hands-on approach to governance, working closely with each vertical to drive growth and operational efficiency.

Express Airways is the proud sponsor of Walter Dog's Game Spot, supporting the gaming community through strategic partnership and brand-level investment.
