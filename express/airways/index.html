About

Corporate Profile

A diversified holding company with a focus on long-term value creation

Express Airways is a private holding company that builds, acquires, and operates businesses across technology, media, logistics, and gaming. The company was founded with a mission to identify and nurture high-potential enterprises, providing them with strategic capital, operational expertise, and access to a global network.

Through its portfolio of wholly owned and majority-held subsidiaries, Express Airways operates across more than 30 countries, employing over 5,000 people. The holding company takes a hands-on approach to governance, working closely with subsidiary management teams to drive growth, operational efficiency, and market expansion.

Express Airways is the proud sponsor of Walter Dog's Game Spot, supporting the gaming community through brand-level investment and strategic partnership.
