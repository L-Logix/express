3. Luggage and Baggage

Carry-on baggage must not exceed 7kg (15lbs). Checked baggage allowance is 23kg (50lbs) per passenger for Economy class, 32kg (70lbs) for Business and First Class.

4. Flight Changes

Flight changes are permitted up to 2 hours before departure. Change fees apply based on fare class and destination.

Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

We collect personal information including name, email address, phone number, passport details, payment information, and travel preferences when you book a flight or create an account.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to process bookings, provide customer support, improve our services, send flight updates, and comply with legal obligations.

Data Sharing

We may share your information with partner airlines, airport authorities, customs and immigration officials, and payment processors as necessary for your travel.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active and for a period of 5 years thereafter to comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. We will notify you of material changes via email or through our website.
